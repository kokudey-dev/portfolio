KD
Ghana 🇬🇭 Mobile App Developer UI/UX Branding Photography Web Dev Videography

Designing & building from first principles.

I'm a product designer and developer based in Ghana, working at the intersection of thoughtful design and purposeful engineering. My work spans mobile applications, web experiences, and visual identities, always rooted in solving real problems for real people.

When I'm not writing code or pushing pixels, I'm helping run ventures under M&M Technologies, growing Kefa Engineering Services across West Africa, and partnering at Ace of Africa Studio in Kumasi.

Outside of work, I teach Sunday school, I enjoy taking photos and videos, and also help shape sermon series at The Apostolic Church, Ghana, Tema C5 Youth Family, because good communication, whether spiritual or digital, changes how people see the world.
